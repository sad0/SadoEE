SadoEE v1.0.1

Party Stash and SadoQoL in one mod: a shared infinite stash on a redesigned inventory page, a rest camp with its merchant, scholar and lich, a party of up to 32, and many quality-of-life tweaks. Every feature is an optional component.

Installation

  1. Install EEex.
  2. Extract the archive into the game folder (where chitin.key is).
  3. Run setup-SadoEE.exe, pick your language and the components you want.
  4. Start the game with InfinityLoader.exe (EEex).

Coming from Party Stash or SadoQoL

SadoEE replaces both mods (same files): uninstall every Party Stash and SadoQoL component first, then install SadoEE. Your saves keep working: the stash contents, the camp and its people keep their names.

In game

The gold ? button on the inventory page explains every part of the stash page. The camp is reached from the world map: the parchment above the Travel button.

Uninstalling

Empty the stash first, in game: Ctrl + left click on the big bag next to the weight. Otherwise its contents stay in your saves but cannot be reached until the stash is installed again. With the big party, bring the party back to 6 members or fewer before uninstalling #40.
